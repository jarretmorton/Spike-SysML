Maximum-speed wall approach — telemetry

Ten runs on a LEGO SPIKE Prime rover. Orange is the raw ultrasonic; blue is the fused sensor+encoder estimate; grey marks the brake command. Distances in mm, time in ms since launch. Traces are the downsampled series retrieved from the hub after each run.

Characterization 1 — raw min(A,B) vs fused estimate

First full-speed probe. The raw channel shows a 2000 mm dropout at ~830 ms, several high spikes, and a 220 ms freeze at 590 mm. The fused estimate absorbs all but the dropout. Braked at 400 mm threshold, settled 333 mm.

Operation attempt 2 (program v1) — 350 ms freeze

Sensor A held 590 mm for 350 ms, blind through 169 mm of travel. Dead-reckoning predicted 421 mm; the sensor woke at 411. The two spikes to 285 mm after the stop are why the final figure is a 41-sample median. Settled 78 mm.

Operation attempt 3 (program v1) — escape-rule failure

The 595 mm freeze ran 483 ms. At 400 ms the re-anchor escape fired and latched onto the STALE 595 value, throwing the estimate 193 mm backwards and restarting the staleness clock. Real readings at 353 mm then sat 180 mm outside the gate: 15 consecutive lock-outs. The blind brake caught it at 147 mm. No contact.

The dangerous fault class: sensor A latched a FALSE 785 mm while true distance was 588 mm and falling, holding the lie 350 ms. A high reading delays braking. The gate rejected it outright; recovery at 411 vs 419 predicted. Settled 84 mm (measured 271 mm).
